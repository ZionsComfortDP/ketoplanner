Add it to your home screen

Your planner lives at the web address below. Just open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen — no files to download, no apps to install.

Your Planner Link

https://zionscomfortdp.github.io/ketoplanner/

1

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Safari and go to the link above.
  2. The planner should load right away, with working tabs across the top (Meal Library, Weekly Planner, Grocery List, Favorites).
  3. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up).
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, name it (e.g. "Meal Planner"), then tap Add.
The icon opens the planner full-screen with no browser bars — just like a regular app.
2

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome and go to the link above.
  2. The planner should load right away, with working tabs across the top.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right of Chrome).
  4. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.
Good to know: on Android, this creates a shortcut that still launches inside Chrome (with the browser address bar visible), rather than a fully standalone app window. It's still one tap to open, and everything works exactly the same — the difference is cosmetic.

About your data

Everything you enter — meals, your weekly plan, and your grocery list — saves automatically on that device as you go. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Use the Export button inside the app any time to download a backup file, and Import to load it back in — handy for moving your data to a new phone or keeping an extra copy.
